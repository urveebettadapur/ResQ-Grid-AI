1. Problem Statement Slide 01 / 07

Disaster Response Breakdown

The Life-or-Death Bottleneck in Emergency Coordination

🚨 Helpline Congestion & Blackouts

During floods and cyclones, cellular towers fail. Traditional 112 emergency helplines get overwhelmed by thousands of unstructured calls with 0% geospatial visibility.

🌊 Static Maps Fail in Dynamic Floods

Standard GPS navigation apps do not track rising flood depths, collapsed bridges, or submerged transformers, causing rescue boats and ambulances to get trapped.

⏳ The Lost "Golden Hour"

First responders lack explainable triage priority. Critical infants, pregnant mothers, and hemorrhage trauma victims are delayed while minor requests are processed.
